Automatic scans

Normal scans process the oldest unscanned story messages first in smaller bounded batches. The extraction pass may take up to 6 minutes; a malformed response can use one additional repair pass of up to 3 minutes. If extraction fails, automatic retries pause until you press Scan Now or Reset Scan.

Structured NPC hints (any preset)

For presets that do not already emit structured NPC data. StoryState adds a small hidden instruction to normal narrator requests, consumes compact [SS:NPC|...] metadata locally, then removes the hint block from the saved reply. No extra model call. Major/Support hints may add or fill character identity fields; Minor hints never create a new character; manual corrections are never overwritten; relationship hints never directly change relationship scores.

Pura adapter (optional)

Use this instead of StoryState-native hints when your preset already emits Pura [NPC:...] tags. StoryState remains authoritative: Major/Support profile tags may add or fill character identity fields, Minor tags never create a new character, manual corrections are never overwritten, and [NPC:REL] text never directly changes relationship scores.

World arcs & events

When enabled, normal StoryState scans may create or update only finite unresolved threads actually established in the saved story. Empty arc slots are allowed: StoryState never auto-repopulates arcs just because one resolves. At most two active arcs are selectively supplied to the narrator, with stronger anti-stall guidance only when an arc is relevant or approaching resolution.
